What is distinctive about ICSE, concretely

People say ICSE is "harder" without saying how. Here is how, for my three subjects.

More examined science. Physics, Chemistry and Biology are three separate 80-mark papers, so 240 marks of written science against CBSE's single 80-mark paper. The three scores are then averaged into one Science mark for the result, which means strong Physics and Chemistry genuinely pull up a weaker Biology.

Shorter papers. Two hours for Physics and Chemistry, two and a half for Maths. CBSE gives three. Pace is a real and separate skill.

More numerical. Mole concept and stoichiometry sits in the ICSE Class 10 board syllabus with full volumetric calculation, where CBSE treats it lightly in Class 9. Electrolysis, metallurgy processes and analytical chemistry have no CBSE Class 10 equivalent at all. In Physics, Machines, Calorimetry and Radioactivity are ICSE-only.

Different maths. ICSE Class 10 has Matrices, Loci, GST, Banking, Shares and Dividends and Geometric Progression. And ICSE Class 9 teaches Logarithms, which CBSE does not teach in Class 9 or 10 at all.

Longer answers. ICSE rewards descriptive answers with explicit reasoning, and in Chemistry expects balanced equations written with conditions and reagents. CBSE marking is more keyword-driven. Writing a complete ICSE answer is a trained habit, not a natural one.

Questions parents ask

ICSE tuition — what parents ask me

Do you teach Biology?

No — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. In ICSE that works out neatly, because the three science paper scores are averaged into one Science mark. Strong Physics and Chemistry lift the average, and a separate Biology teacher handles the third paper. Most of my ICSE families run it exactly that way.

Which books do you use?

Selina Concise for all three subjects, and Dalal's Simplified Chemistry alongside for equation and objective drill. That is the standard ICSE combination — Selina for theory, Dalal for practice — although CISCE itself prescribes no textbook for any subject. If your school uses something different, we use that.

Is ICSE being changed this year?

Not for Physics, Chemistry or Mathematics in any way I can confirm from a CISCE document. There has been no syllabus reduction announced for 2026-27. The twice-a-year Class 10 board exam that is in the news is a CBSE change; CISCE has announced nothing of the kind and ICSE remains a single annual board examination.

Is ICSE a disadvantage for JEE and NEET?

A small one, and it is manageable. Those exams follow the NCERT line, so a CBSE student is already reading the right books. But ICSE students usually arrive stronger in numerical work, in writing out a full solution and in a few topics CBSE skips — mole concept, electrolysis, machines. The gaps against NCERT are identifiable and we teach them separately. It is not a reason to change schools.
