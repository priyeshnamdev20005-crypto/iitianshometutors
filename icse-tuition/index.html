Why ICSE trips up good students

ICSE students are usually better readers and better writers than their state-board counterparts, and then they hit Class 9 Physics and stall. The reason is simple: ICSE expects numerical work and stated reasoning much earlier. A question does not just ask what happens, it asks why, with the derivation, in your own words, with units.

That is a skill, not a talent, and it has to be taught deliberately. In a school class of forty there is no time to watch each child's working and correct how they set a problem up. That is exactly what one-to-one classes are for.

I work from the books your child already has — Selina / Concise for Physics and Maths and Viraf Dalal for Chemistry are what most schools here follow — so there is no parallel syllabus to carry.

Class 8 and 9 are the quiet years that decide Class 10

Nobody panics in Class 8. That is precisely why it is the cheapest year to fix things. The algebra that makes Class 10 Maths feel impossible, and the numerical habit that makes Class 11 Physics feel impossible, are both built here.

It is also the right window for anything beyond the syllabus. If your child is quick and bored, olympiads are a much better use of that energy than extra worksheets — see the olympiad page. And if the long-term target is engineering or medicine, the JEE and NEET Foundation track starts in exactly these years.

Questions parents ask

ICSE tuition — what parents ask me

Which books do you follow?

Whatever your child's school follows — in practice that is usually Selina / Concise for Physics and Maths and Viraf Dalal for Chemistry. I do not run a parallel syllabus; the school's book stays the main book and I add practice on top.

Do you teach ICSE Biology?

No. Physics, Chemistry and Maths only. Biology needs a Biology teacher and I will say so plainly rather than take the subject.

Do you teach Class 10 ICSE as well?

Class 8 and 9 are what I take regularly. If you have a Class 10 ICSE student, call me and tell me the subjects and where the gaps are — I will tell you honestly whether I am the right person for that year, and if not I will say so.

My child does well in tests but loses marks in exams. Why?

Almost always presentation. ICSE gives marks for the statement, the working, the diagram and the units — a right answer with no steps still loses. We practise writing answers the way they are marked, which often moves a paper by ten marks with no new learning at all.

Can we start mid-year?

Yes. We start with a diagnostic — a short test to find what is actually missing — and begin from the real gap rather than from wherever the class happens to be.
