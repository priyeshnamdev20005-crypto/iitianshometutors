Which page should I open first?
The one that names your child's board and class. If your child is in ICSE Class 9, open ICSE Class 9 — it lists the actual chapters and the actual paper pattern. If you are choosing between boards for Class 10, open the comparison page instead.
Do you teach all of these yourself?
Yes. There is no panel of teachers behind this and no assistant taking the class when I am busy. That is also why I say plainly that I do not teach NEET Biology.
