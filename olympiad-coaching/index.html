Why olympiads are worth the time

School exams test whether your child has learnt the chapter. Olympiad papers test whether your child can use the chapter on a problem they have never seen. Those are different abilities, and only the second one is any use in Class 11, in JEE, or frankly in life.

There is a practical benefit too. A student who has spent Class 8 and 9 wrestling with olympiad problems finds Class 11 Physics ordinary rather than terrifying, because being stuck is already a familiar feeling instead of a sign of failure.

I should add the honest caveat: olympiads suit the curious child. If your child is currently fighting to keep up with school, the right answer is to fix that first — and I will tell you so.

The method

Problems, not chapters

A class is three or four good problems, not a lecture. Your child works; I ask questions. The aim is for them to find the idea, because an idea you were told is forgotten and an idea you found is not.

The point

Being stuck is the lesson

We spend real time stuck, deliberately. A student who has never been stuck for twenty minutes panics in Class 11. A student who has learnt to sit with a hard problem simply keeps working.

The payoff

Where it pays off

Olympiad work maps directly onto JEE Advanced-style questions — multi-concept, no obvious method. Foundation students who do olympiads reach JEE preparation with that already in place.

What I teach, and what I don't

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. In an olympiad paper that has a Biology section — NSO and NSTSE do — I take the Physics and Chemistry portion and leave Biology to a Biology teacher. That is stated plainly so you are not surprised later.

For most families olympiad work sits alongside regular classes rather than replacing them: school syllabus in the main classes, one olympiad session a week for the fun of it. That runs naturally with the Foundation track for Class 8, 9 and 10, or alongside SSC and ICSE tuition.

Questions parents ask

Olympiads — what parents ask me

Which olympiad should we start with?

For most students, SOF IMO or NSTSE — they are school-registered, familiar in format, and a gentle introduction. If your child takes to it, IOQM is the next real step in Maths. Std 8 students in Maharashtra should also look at the MSCE scholarship exam, and Std 9 at Homi Bhabha.

Will olympiad preparation hurt school marks?

It should not, and if it starts to, we stop and rebalance. School comes first. One olympiad class a week, on top of a student who is comfortable with school work, does not damage anything — it usually helps, because the school questions start looking easy.

Do you prepare for NTSE?

No, because NTSE has been on hold since 2021-22 and there is nothing to prepare for. KVPY has likewise been merged into INSPIRE. If a class is still selling NTSE coaching, ask them what exam your child will actually sit.

Do you teach the Biology part of NSO or NSTSE?

No. I take the Physics and Chemistry portion. Biology needs a Biology teacher.

My child is good at Maths but not interested in competitions. Should we push?

I would not. Olympiads only work when the child wants the problems; pushed students quit within a month and often lose interest in the subject too. Try one free class and let your child decide — you will get a clear answer.
