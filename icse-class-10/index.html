The ICSE paper, honestly described

ICSE Class 10 asks for more written science than any other Indian board. Physics, Chemistry and Biology are three separate 80-mark papers — 240 marks of examined science, where CBSE sets one 80-mark Science paper. And the ICSE papers are shorter: two hours for Physics and Chemistry, two and a half for Maths, against CBSE's three.

So the pressure is real and it is mostly about pace and precision. Then there is the part nobody trains: Section B is a choice. Physics and Chemistry give you six questions and ask for four; Maths gives seven and asks for four. Deciding which four, during the fifteen minutes of reading time, is worth marks on its own — and it is a decision most students make badly, by starting the first question they recognise.

One structural thing parents often miss: the three science paper scores are averaged into a single Science mark out of 100 for the result and the best-of-five percentage. So strong Physics and Chemistry genuinely pull up a weaker Biology.

Neither is 'better'ICSE suits a student who writes well and enjoys depth; CBSE suits a student aiming at JEE or NEET, because the NCERT line matches those exams. Both produce good engineers. The board matters far less than whether the child is actually doing the work.
The board-year plan

How the ICSE Class 10 year runs

The syllabus is finished early on purpose, because the last three months belong to papers.

  1. Finish the syllabus by DecemberSelina end to end, with Dalal for chemistry drill. Nothing is left for January — January is for papers.
  2. Numericals until they are boringMole concept, electrolysis calculations, machines, calorimetry, refraction. ICSE Physics and Chemistry are numerical papers and this is where marks actually move.
  3. Full solution writingBalanced equations with conditions and reagents. Complete reasoning written out. ICSE examiners mark what is on the page, and short answers score short.
  4. Practical and internal workTwenty marks per subject from real lab work — salt analysis, gas tests, least count and error handling. It is the easiest 20 marks on the paper and the most casually lost.
  5. Past papers under two hoursReal ICSE papers, timed, marked by hand. Ten years of them by the end. The same questions come back in different clothes.
  6. Section B drillsPractising the choice itself — reading six questions in the first five minutes and picking four. A skill, not a talent.
Best of fiveEnglish is always counted, plus your child's four next-highest subjects, out of 500. A subject excluded from the percentage still has to be passed.

Questions parents ask

ICSE Class 10 tuition — what parents ask me

Do you teach Biology for the ICSE science group?

No. I teach Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Since ICSE averages the three science paper scores into one Science mark, strong Physics and Chemistry pull the average up and a separate Biology teacher handles the third paper. That is how most of my ICSE students run it.

How long is the ICSE Maths paper, and is there internal assessment?

Two and a half hours for 80 marks, plus 20 marks of internal assessment, plus fifteen minutes of reading time. You will find websites saying ICSE Maths is a three-hour paper with no internal assessment — both are wrong, and they have been copied around a lot. The CISCE syllabus wording is two and a half hours and 20 internal marks.

What is the most common reason ICSE students lose marks in Chemistry?

Writing an equation without conditions or reagents, and answering a Study of Compounds question in general terms instead of describing the actual laboratory preparation. Both are entirely fixable, and both come from practising with a solution book instead of writing answers out.

Does ICSE also have two board exams a year now, like CBSE?

No. That is a CBSE change and CISCE has announced nothing of the kind. ICSE remains a single annual board examination. CISCE did run a two-semester ICSE in 2021-22 as a COVID measure and went back to one examination afterwards.

Is it too late to start in Class 10?

Not if you start now rather than in December. Two of my three subjects are heavily numerical, and numerical marks move faster than descriptive ones — a student who drills mole concept, electrolysis and machines properly can shift a Chemistry or Physics score noticeably in a term. What cannot be fixed in six weeks is Class 9 algebra, so tell me honestly where the gaps are on the first call.
