Why Std 9 decides Std 10

Parents call me in October of Class 10. Almost every time, the problem started a year earlier. The Std 10 Balbharati books are written on the assumption that Std 9 is finished and solid — they do not reteach it. Similarity in Std 10 Geometry assumes your child can handle triangle properties and ratios cold. Std 10 Trigonometry assumes the ratios and the standard-angle table from Std 9 are already memorised. Std 10 Chemistry assumes the mole concept from Measurement of Matter.

A student who never really closed those Std 9 chapters does not fail Std 10. They just spend the whole year working twice as hard for the same marks, and they never find out why.

One more thing worth knowing: 2026-27 is the last year of the current Std 9 Balbharati books. Maharashtra's NEP-aligned textbook rollout brings new Std 9 books in 2027-28. Everything on this page is the syllabus your child is studying now.

The Std 9 exam, and what it is not

Std 9 is not a board exam. The paper is set and marked by your child's own school, and most Maharashtra schools mirror the SSC shape — Algebra and Geometry as separate papers, Science with a practical or oral component. The board publishes no official Std 9 paper pattern, so if a coaching class shows you an "official Std 9 blueprint", treat it carefully.

What this really means is that the Std 9 marksheet is not the point. The point is whether the chapters are closed. A student can score 85% in a soft school paper and still walk into Std 10 unable to prove two triangles similar.

Questions parents ask

Class 9 SSC tuition — what parents ask me

Is Class 9 Science really one book, or two?

One. Std 9 Science and Technology is a single Balbharati book of eighteen chapters. The Part 1 and Part 2 split begins in Std 10. Parents ask me this constantly because the Std 10 books are the ones everybody has heard of.

My child scores well in school but I am still worried. Is tuition needed?

Maybe not. Send me the last Geometry and Science papers, or just ask your child to prove two triangles congruent and explain which test they used and why. If that answer is fluent, you probably do not need me yet. If it is a guess dressed up as an answer, the marks are coming from a friendly paper rather than from understanding.

Do you teach Biology?

No. Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics only. I would rather tell you that on the first call than take the subject, teach it out of a guide book and waste your child's year. For Biology you keep a separate teacher, and plenty of my students do exactly that.

Do you teach in Marathi medium or English medium?

Both. The books are the same syllabus in either medium, and I teach in English while explaining in Marathi or Hindi wherever that makes the idea clear. Semi-English students — English for Maths and Science, Marathi for the rest — are the most common case in this area and are completely fine.

The Std 9 books are changing next year. Does that affect my child?

Only if your child repeats the year. Maharashtra's new NEP-aligned Std 9 books arrive in 2027-28, so a student in Std 9 during 2026-27 finishes on the current books and then sits Std 10 on the current Std 10 books. The Std 10 changeover is expected later and the exact year has not been confirmed by an official board circular, so I will not put a date on it.
