The SSC paper is a speed exam in disguise

Here is the fact most students only discover in the exam hall: an SSC Algebra or Geometry paper is forty marks in two hours, and so is Science Part 1. That sounds generous until you see the paper — a block of one-mark objective questions, then two-, three-, four- and five-mark questions, with every step of the working carrying marks.

There is no negative marking, which changes the strategy completely: every blank is a wasted mark, and an attempted answer with correct working scores even when the final number is wrong. Half the marks I recover for students come from teaching them to write the steps they already know instead of leaving the question.

The board sets its paper from the Balbharati textbook. Not from a guide, not from a reference book. So we finish the textbook — every practice set — and then move to board papers.

The four papers I teach

Chapter by chapter

Algebra, Geometry and Science Part 1. Science Part 2 stays with a Biology teacher.

40 marks

Algebra — Mathematics Part 1

Six chapters, 40 marks.

  • Linear Equations in Two Variables
  • Quadratic Equations
  • Arithmetic Progression
  • Financial Planning (GST, shares)
  • Probability
  • Statistics

Financial Planning and Statistics are the two chapters students leave for last and then lose easy marks in. We do them early.

40 marks

Geometry — Mathematics Part 2

Seven chapters, 40 marks. The proof-writing paper.

  • Similarity
  • Pythagoras Theorem
  • Circle
  • Geometric Constructions
  • Co-ordinate Geometry
  • Trigonometry
  • Mensuration

Similarity alone carries more weight than any other chapter. Constructions are pure marks if the arcs are left visible — a thing students are never told.

40 marks

Science and Technology Part 1

Ten chapters, 40 marks. Cleanly Physics and Chemistry, which is why this is my paper.

  • Gravitation · Effects of Electric Current · Heat
  • Refraction of Light · Lenses · Space Missions
  • Periodic Classification · Chemical Reactions and Equations
  • Metallurgy · Carbon Compounds
Not taught here

Science and Technology Part 2

Not my paper, and I will say why plainly. Part 2 is mostly Biology — Life Processes, Heredity and Evolution, Animal Classification, Microbiology, Cell Biology.

It does also carry Towards Green Energy, Environmental Management, Social Health and Disaster Management, which are not Biology — so calling Part 2 “the Biology paper” is not quite right either. Keep a Biology teacher for it.

The board-exam routine

How the Std 10 year runs

Nothing exotic. The difficulty is doing it every week from June instead of every day from January.

  1. Finish the textbook firstEvery practice set in the Balbharati book, in order. The board writes its paper from this book, so this is not optional work — it is the syllabus.
  2. Chapter test, written under timeAfter each chapter, a paper in board format and board handwriting. Marked by hand with the lost marks circled and explained.
  3. Then previous board papersOnce the book is done, we move to real SSC papers. Students discover the same questions come back in slightly different clothes.
  4. Presentation, deliberatelySteps written out, diagrams labelled, construction arcs left on the page, the final statement written. Examiners give marks for what they can see.
  5. Speed workTimed sets to fit forty marks into two hours comfortably rather than exactly. Finishing with ten minutes spare is a skill we build, not luck.
  6. Weekly parent updateWhat was covered, the test score, and the honest sentence about what is still not working.
Attempt everythingNo negative marking means a blank is the only guaranteed zero. I train students to write the setup and the formula even when the final answer will not come.

Questions parents ask

Class 10 SSC tuition — what parents ask me

Do you teach Science Part 2?

No. Part 2 is mostly Biology — Life Processes, Heredity and Evolution, Animal Classification, Microbiology and Cell Biology. I teach Physics, Chemistry and Maths. Part 2 also carries Towards Green Energy, Environmental Management, Social Health and Disaster Management, which are not Biology, but the paper as a whole belongs with a Biology teacher and I will not pretend otherwise.

Is there negative marking in the SSC board exam?

No. Not in any SSC paper. That is why I insist on attempting every question — writing the formula and the setup earns marks even when the final answer does not come out, and a blank earns nothing at all.

How long is each paper?

Two hours for Algebra, Geometry, Science Part 1 and Science Part 2 — these are 40-mark papers. You will see “3 hours” on some websites; that figure belongs to the 100-mark language papers and has been copied across carelessly. We practise at two hours.

My child is weak in Geometry. Is that fixable in Std 10?

Usually yes, and usually faster than parents expect, because Geometry marks are lost to presentation as often as to understanding. Similarity is the chapter to attack first — it is the largest, and it is where a student who cannot write a proof properly loses marks they had actually earned in their head.

Is the Maharashtra Std 10 syllabus changing?

Not for your child right now. The current Std 10 books and the current SSC pattern are in force for 2026-27. Maharashtra is rolling out NEP-aligned books class by class — Std 9 changes in 2027-28 — and Std 10 follows later. Reports disagree on the exact year and no official circular settles it, so I will not put a date on it.
