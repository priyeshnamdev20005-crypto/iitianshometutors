Who this is for

Two kinds of families, and they want slightly different things.

Indian families living abroad — mostly in the Gulf, but also the UK and further — whose children are on CBSE or ICSE at an Indian school overseas, or who plan to come back for Class 11 and 12. The worry is usually the same: the school is fine but the Physics and Maths are thin, and there is no one nearby who teaches the Indian syllabus the way it is actually examined.

Families in India outside Mira Bhayandar — anywhere in Mumbai, Maharashtra or the rest of the country. Same classes, same material; the only thing missing is my travel time, which often means the class can be scheduled more flexibly.

How an online class runs

What the class actually looks like

Not a video lecture. A live one-to-one class where your child writes and I watch.

  • Live one-to-one on Zoom or Google Meet — never a recording
  • I write by hand on a digital pad, so your child sees the working build up
  • Your child solves on paper and shows me the page — I correct the method, not just the answer
  • Chapter notes and short notes sent as PDF, ready to print at your end
  • DPP after every class, sent the same day
  • A marked test every Sunday — sent, written, photographed back, corrected by me
  • Doubts on WhatsApp between classes, answered in writing
  • A weekly update to parents, whatever the time difference

What I teach online

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, Class 8 to 12, on CBSE, ICSE or the Maharashtra state syllabus — and the exam preparation that sits on top: JEE Main and Advanced, NEET Physics and Chemistry, MHT-CET and olympiads. Younger students usually start on the Foundation track.

Biology I do not teach, online or otherwise.

What you need at your end

A laptop or tablet, a decent internet line, and paper and pen. That is genuinely it — no app to buy, no tablet required. A printer helps for the notes, but families without one just read them on screen and write in their own notebook.

Fees and payment

Fees for overseas families are quoted per month rather than per hour, once we have agreed the number of classes a week. Indian families can pay by UPI or bank transfer. If you are outside India, ask me on WhatsApp what payment options are available for your country — I would rather tell you what is actually working today than list something on a website.

The first class is free wherever you are.

Questions parents ask

Online tuition — what parents ask me

Is an online class really as good as a teacher sitting next to my child?

For a motivated student, close to it — and for a distracted one, slightly worse. The honest difference is not the teaching, it is the accountability of a person in the room. I compensate with the Sunday test and a weekly parent update, so nothing quietly drifts for a month.

Which syllabus can you teach for a child studying abroad?

CBSE and ICSE, which is what most Indian schools overseas follow, and the Maharashtra state syllabus. If your child is on a British or American curriculum, tell me the board and the topics before you book — I will look at it honestly and say no if it is not my syllabus.

How do the notes reach us?

As PDFs on WhatsApp or email, the same notes my local students get printed. Print them if you can; if not, your child reads on screen and writes in their own notebook.

How does the Sunday test work at a distance?

I send the paper at a fixed time, your child writes it on paper under time, photographs it and sends it back. I mark it by hand, write out the corrections and send it back with a short note on what to fix. We then go over the mistakes in the next class.

Can you hold the same slot every week with the time difference?

That is the main thing to settle before we start, so tell me your city and the times that suit you. I will only commit to a slot I can actually keep every week — an unreliable class is worse than none, especially across time zones.

What are your fees for overseas students?

Quoted monthly, based on the class, the subjects and how many classes a week. WhatsApp me on +91 79774 75848 with your child's class, board and your city and I will give you a figure and the payment options for your country.
