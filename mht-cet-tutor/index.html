Why CET needs its own preparation

Parents often assume that JEE preparation automatically covers MHT-CET. It mostly does on syllabus, and then the exam behaves completely differently on the day.

Three differences decide marks. First, no negative marking — so an unattempted question is a pure loss and guessing has a real place, which is the opposite of what JEE trains. Second, the paper is built on the Maharashtra state board syllabus, so the phrasing, the standard results and even the numbers feel like the Balbharati textbook rather than NCERT. Third, it is a speed exam: a large number of questions in a short time, which means the student who solves in three steps beats the student who solves in seven.

A JEE-trained student who walks into CET without adjusting will leave questions blank and over-solve the easy ones. That is the gap I close.

How we prepare

Three habits CET rewards

Free marks

Attempt everything

With no negative marking, a blank is the only guaranteed zero. We build the discipline of never leaving a question, plus real elimination technique so a guess is an educated one.

Speed

Solve in fewer steps

Standard results, direct formulae and option-checking are taught as legitimate tools. On a speed paper, the elegant three-line method beats the rigorous full derivation.

Accuracy

Stay on the state syllabus

We work from the Balbharati textbook line and the state-board phrasing, then add practice. A CET question usually looks like a textbook question with one twist.

Boards, CET and JEE together

Most of my Class 12 students are sitting more than one exam. The good news is that the state board and CET share a syllabus, so the work compounds if it is planned. The board exam gives the base; CET adds speed and MCQ technique on the same chapters; JEE, if your child is also attempting it, adds depth.

We plan it in that order rather than running three separate preparations — that is how students end up doing three times the work for one result. If JEE is the main target, see the JEE page. Questions parents ask

MHT-CET tuition — what parents ask me

Is MHT-CET easier than JEE?

The questions are easier; getting a high percentile is not. Because there is no negative marking and the syllabus is the state board's, a very large number of students score well — so the cut-off for a good college sits high. Easy questions plus a high cut-off means accuracy and speed matter more than cleverness.

Do you teach Biology for PCB students?

No, Physics, Chemistry and Maths only. For PCB students I take Physics and Chemistry and you keep a separate Biology teacher.

When should CET preparation start?

Class 11 ideally, because about a fifth of the paper comes from Class 11 and that portion is the one students forget. Practically, most families start in Class 12 — that works, provided we schedule Class 11 revision into the year instead of hoping for a miracle in April.

My child is doing JEE coaching. Is separate CET preparation really needed?

Not a whole separate course, but yes, separate practice. JEE-trained students consistently lose CET marks by leaving questions blank and by over-solving. A few weeks of CET-pattern timed papers and state-syllabus alignment fixes that, and I build it into the normal classes.

Do you cover the Class 11 portion as well?

Yes. It is about 20% of the paper and it is almost always the weak part, so Class 11 revision is scheduled through the Class 12 year rather than left to the end.
