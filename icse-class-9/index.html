The trap in ICSE Class 9

ICSE Class 9 is taught on a syllabus set by CISCE, but examined by your child's own school. So the syllabus is genuinely demanding — gas laws, atomic structure and bonding, the mid-point theorem, logarithms, trigonometric ratios of standard angles — while the paper that measures it is set by a teacher who wants the class to do well.

A student can therefore end Class 9 with 82% and no working command of logarithms, and nobody finds out until the Class 10 board paper. ICSE Class 10 does not reteach Class 9. It assumes it, silently, in almost every chapter.

My job in Class 9 is unfashionable: make sure the chapter is actually finished, with numericals done on paper, before the school moves on.

The Class 9 syllabus

Officially, and in the books

CISCE sets the sections; Selina and Dalal are the books almost every school here uses.

Numericals throughout

Physics — 8 official sections

Measurements and Experimentation; Motion in One Dimension; Laws of Motion; Fluids; Heat and Energy; Light; Sound; Electricity and Magnetism.

Selina Concise Physics 9 runs these as ten chapters, splitting Fluids into pressure/atmospheric pressure and upthrust/Archimedes/floatation, and Electricity and Magnetism into Current Electricity and Magnetism.

Two hours, 80 marks, plus 20 marks of practical work.

Selina + Dalal

Chemistry — 8 official sections

The Language of Chemistry; Chemical Changes and Reactions; Water; Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding; The Periodic Table; Study of the First Element — Hydrogen; Study of Gas Laws; Atmospheric Pollution.

Selina Concise Chemistry 9 maps one-to-one onto this list — the cleanest textbook-to-syllabus match at this level. Dalal's Simplified Chemistry 9 is the second book, used for equation drill and objective work.

28 chapters

Mathematics — 8 official units

Pure Arithmetic; Commercial Mathematics; Algebra; Geometry; Statistics; Mensuration; Trigonometry; Co-ordinate Geometry.

Selina Concise Mathematics 9 breaks these into 28 chapters. Two and a half hours, 80 marks, plus 20 internal.

The chapters that decide the year: Expansions, Factorisation, Indices, Logarithms, Triangles and congruency, Mid-point Theorem, Pythagoras, Trigonometrical Ratios of Standard Angles.

How a week runs

The Class 9 routine

Same shape as my board-year students, at a calmer pace.

  1. Concept classOne topic at a time, worked on paper. Your child holds the pen — I am not going to lecture at a fourteen-year-old for forty minutes.
  2. Selina exercises completelyAll of them, not the selected sums. The school sets its questions from this book and so does everyone else.
  3. Dalal for chemistry drillEquations, laboratory preparations and objective questions, which is where Dalal is stronger than Selina.
  4. Numericals on paperPhysics and Chemistry numericals written out in full, with units. ICSE marks the working, so the working is what we practise.
  5. Practical work taken seriouslyTwenty marks of Physics and twenty of Chemistry come from practical work — least count, significant figures, error handling. Cheap marks that students throw away.
  6. Chapter test, marked by handWritten under time, corrections written out rather than a score scribbled at the top.
Answer length is a skillICSE rewards longer descriptive answers with explicit reasoning, unlike CBSE's keyword-driven marking. Writing a complete answer is trained, not natural.

Questions parents ask

ICSE Class 9 tuition — what parents ask me

Which books do you teach from?

Selina Concise for all three subjects, because that is what almost every ICSE school here prescribes, and Dalal's Simplified Chemistry alongside it for equation and objective practice. Selina for the theory, Dalal for the drill — that is the standard ICSE combination, though I should say plainly that CISCE prescribes no textbook for any subject. If your school uses something else, we use that.

Is ICSE really harder than CBSE at Class 9?

Different rather than simply harder, but in Physics, Chemistry and Maths it is genuinely heavier. ICSE Class 9 Maths teaches logarithms, which CBSE does not teach in Class 9 or 10 at all. ICSE Chemistry does gas laws in Class 9. And ICSE examines Physics and Chemistry as separate 80-mark papers rather than one combined Science paper. More content, more numericals, longer written answers.

Do you teach Biology?

No. Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics only. I would rather tell you that on the first call than take the subject, teach it out of a guide book and waste your child's year. For Biology you keep a separate teacher, and plenty of my students do exactly that.

Do the Class 9 marks matter for the Class 10 board result?

Not directly — the ICSE board result is built from the Class 10 papers. What Class 9 decides is how much of Class 10 is new learning versus repair work. A student who genuinely closed Class 9 spends Class 10 going forward.

Has the ICSE syllabus changed for this year?

Not for Physics, Chemistry or Mathematics in any way I can confirm. CISCE has published regulations and syllabuses for the 2027 and 2028 examination years, and there has been no syllabus reduction announced for 2026-27. There is reporting about competency-based questions rising over the next few years, but I have not seen that in a circular I could read, so I will not state it as fact. What I can say plainly: the twice-a-year board exam you may have heard about is a CBSE change. CISCE has announced nothing of the kind.
