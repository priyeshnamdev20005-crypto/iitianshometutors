Chapter notes

Full notes for every chapter, written by me in a notebook layout — diagrams redrawn, every topic on its own page. The student reads them like their own notebook, not a photocopy.

Daily practice problems

DPP after every class

A problem sheet goes home after each class, on exactly what we just did. Practice happens the same day — not the night before the test.

Test every Sunday

A full paper every Sunday on that week’s work. I mark it strictly and the paper comes back with the mistakes marked — so a parent sees real progress, not just attendance.
