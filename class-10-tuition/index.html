What I teach, on every board

Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. Not Biology, not Social Science, not languages — those stay with your child's school or another teacher. On the SSC board that means Algebra, Geometry and Science and Technology Part 1. On CBSE it means Maths and the Physics and Chemistry half of Science. On ICSE it means Maths, Physics and Chemistry as three separate papers.

The classes are one-to-one, at your home, in Mira Road East, Bhayandar East and Bhayandar West — or online if you are elsewhere. Notes, worksheets, question banks and a marked test every week are included in the fees; there is no separate material charge.

Class 9 matters more than you think

Most of the Class 10 problems I am called in to fix started in Class 9, because no board reteaches Class 9 content in Class 10 — it is assumed, silently, in almost every chapter. If your child is in Class 9 now, that is the cheaper year to do this in: SSC Class 9, CBSE Class 9, ICSE Class 9.

And after Class 10

For engineering, the two exams that matter in Maharashtra are MHT-CET and JEE. For medicine, I teach the Physics and Chemistry half of NEET. Questions parents ask

Class 10 tuition — what parents ask me

Which board is best for JEE or NEET?

CBSE has a genuine structural advantage, because JEE and NEET are written on the NCERT line and a CBSE student is already studying those books. That advantage is smaller than people think, though — every year students from SSC and ICSE schools get good ranks. If your child is already in an ICSE or SSC school, changing boards for this reason alone is usually a bad trade.

My child is in an ICSE school but wants to do JEE. Is that a problem?

Not a problem, just a gap to manage. ICSE students usually arrive stronger in numerical work and in writing out a full solution, and weaker on a few NCERT-specific topics that ICSE either does not cover or covers differently. Those are identified and taught separately. The reverse is also true — ICSE Class 10 teaches mole concept, electrolysis and machines at a depth CBSE does not, and that helps later.

Do you teach Biology?

No. Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics only. I would rather tell you that on the first call than take the subject, teach it out of a guide book and waste your child's year. For Biology you keep a separate teacher, and plenty of my students do exactly that.

Is it too late to start in Class 10?

It depends on when in Class 10. From June to about November, plenty can be done. From January, we are doing damage control and I will say so honestly rather than take your money and let you find out in March. Call me and tell me where things stand — if I do not think I can help meaningfully, I will say that.

Do you teach in English, Hindi or Marathi?

Classes are in English, with explanation in Hindi or Marathi wherever that is what makes an idea land. Semi-English students — English for Maths and Science, Marathi or Hindi for the rest — are the most common case in Mira Bhayandar and are completely fine.
