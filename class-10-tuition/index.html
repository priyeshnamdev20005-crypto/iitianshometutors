How Class 10 runs

The week, and the year

Class 10 is not a difficult syllabus. It is a long one, and it punishes falling behind — so the whole method is built around not falling behind.

  1. Chapter classOne chapter at a time with the textbook open. Board questions come from the textbook, so that is where we live, with extra practice on top.
  2. Notes for the chapterMy own handwritten-style notes, printed and given to your child — diagrams, formula boxes, solved examples, the works. Yours to keep.
  3. DPP the same eveningA practice sheet on the day of the class. In Geometry especially, a day's gap undoes the whole class.
  4. Written-answer disciplineMarks in the SSC board go to the steps, the statement and the diagram, not only to the final answer. We practise writing answers the way the examiner wants them.
  5. Test every SundayBoard-pattern, timed, marked by me with corrections written out. By February your child has written more papers than most of the class.
  6. Full board papersComplete board-pattern papers and previous-year sets, done under time in the last stretch.
  7. Parent updateA weekly message with the test score and what is genuinely not solid yet.

Questions parents ask

Class 10 tuition — what parents ask me

Do you teach Science Part 2 (Biology)?

No. I teach Physics, Chemistry and Maths, so in Class 10 that means Algebra, Geometry and Science Part 1. Science Part 2 is Biology and it deserves a Biology teacher. It is also the more readable paper, so many students manage it with school plus my notes for practice.

Can we take only Maths, or only Science?

Yes. Many families start with one subject — usually the one where marks dropped in the first term — and add the second later if it helps. There is no package you are forced into.

Are the notes really included?

Yes, printed and included in the fees. I write them myself for every chapter, in colour, with the diagrams redrawn. If you want to judge them before committing, WhatsApp me a chapter name and I will send that chapter free.

How do the Sunday tests work?

Board pattern, timed, on whatever was covered that week. I mark them by hand and write out the corrections, then we go over the mistakes in the next class. The point is not the score — it is that the same mistake does not survive to February.

My child's Geometry is very weak. Is that fixable in Class 10?

Usually yes, and usually faster than parents expect. Weak Geometry is almost always a Class 9 gap — similarity and basic theorems — rather than a Class 10 problem. We find the gap, fix it, and then the Class 10 chapters stop feeling random.
