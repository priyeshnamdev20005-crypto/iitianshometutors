02Start early

JEE & NEET Foundation, and Olympiads

For students who want to start JEE or NEET preparation in Class 8 or 9. The school syllabus stays first; on top of it, the habit of competitive-level thinking is built slowly — so Class 11 never feels like a jump.

Class 8 · 9 · 10

JEE Foundation

Physics, Chemistry and Maths taken to one level above the school textbook — NCERT first, then foundation-level problems. Concept building, MCQ accuracy, negative-marking discipline and speed, so Class 11 does not feel like a wall.

School marks stay up. The base gets built underneath.

Class 8 · 9 · 10

NEET Foundation — Physics & Chemistry

The Physics and Chemistry half of NEET foundation, taught the same way: NCERT line-by-line, then application questions. Numericals, mole concept, units and measurement — exactly where NEET aspirants lose marks later.

Biology needs a separate teacher — I do not take it.

03Material, not just classes

What every student gets

I write all of it myself, for the chapter we are actually on. Printed and handed over — not links, not someone else’s PDF.

  • Chapter notes

    Handwritten style · printed

    Full notes for every chapter, written by me in a notebook layout — diagrams redrawn, every topic on its own page. The student reads them like their own notebook, not a photocopy.

  • DPP after every class

    Daily practice problems

    A problem sheet goes home after each class, on exactly what we just did. Practice happens the same day — not the night before the test.

  • Test every Sunday

    Marked by me, shown to you

    A full paper every Sunday on that week’s work. I mark it strictly and the paper comes back with the mistakes marked — so a parent sees real progress, not just attendance.

  • PYQ banks

    JEE · MHT-CET · board

    Previous-year questions sorted chapter-wise with step-by-step solutions — JEE Main and Advanced, MHT-CET, and board papers. A student should know what the exam actually asks before sitting it.

  • Short notes & mind maps

    For the last week before the exam

    One-page revision sheets and a mind map per chapter. When there are four days left and the whole syllabus to turn over, this is what a student actually reads.

  • Question banks with answer keys

    Every question type, separately

    MCQ, true-or-false, one-mark, match-the-columns, case-study, diagram-based and numericals — each set with its own answer key. Plus full-length papers in the exact board pattern before the real exam.

06Before you call

Straight answers

What are the fees?
Fees depend on the class, the number of subjects and how many days a week. I will tell you the exact number on the phone in one minute — no packages, no registration fee, no advance. Call 79774 75848.
Is the demo class really free?
Yes. A full class on a real chapter at your home. If you do not want to continue after it, you pay nothing and that is completely fine.
Do you teach Biology?
No. I teach Physics, Chemistry and Maths only. For NEET I take the Physics and Chemistry portion. For Class 10 Science I cover the Physics and Chemistry chapters — for Biology you will need someone else, and I would rather say that upfront.
Is all the material included in the fees?
Yes. Chapter notes, DPP sheets, PYQ banks, short notes, mind maps, question banks and the Sunday test papers — all of it is included. No separate material charge, no printing charge. I write it myself, so there is nothing to sell you on top.
My child is in Class 8 — is it too early for JEE?
No, but it has to be done the right way. At Class 8 and 9 the work is foundation, not coaching: the school syllabus stays first, and on top of it we build concepts, MCQ accuracy and olympiad-style thinking. Nobody should be solving JEE Advanced papers in Class 8 — but a student who builds the base early does not struggle in Class 11.
Which olympiads can you prepare for?
Maths: SOF IMO and the IOQM → RMO → INMO ladder. Science: SOF NSO, NSTSE, IOQJS, and IOQP / IOQC for Class 11–12. Maharashtra papers: Dr. Homi Bhabha Balvaidnyanik (Std 9) and the MSCE Scholarship Exam (Std 8). In any paper with a Biology section I take the Physics and Chemistry portion only.
What timings do you have?
Classes run around school hours, mostly late afternoon to night, and I keep slots on Sunday. Tell me your child's school timing and I will tell you honestly whether I have a slot free.
Do you take online classes?
Yes — one-to-one online, with a writing tablet and screen share so the working is written out like a notebook. I also teach Indian families living abroad whose children follow CBSE or ICSE; Gulf timings suit my evening slots well.
Which areas do you travel to?
Mira Road East, Bhayandar East and Bhayandar West. Beyond that I cannot reach on time, and a tutor who arrives late is no use to anyone — but online is open from anywhere.
How many students do you take at once?
One. Every class is one-to-one, at the student's own home. No batches, no recorded videos, and I teach every class myself.
